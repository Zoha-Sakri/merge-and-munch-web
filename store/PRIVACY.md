# Merge & Munch Privacy Notice

Merge & Munch is designed to work without an account. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on the device or in the browser storage used by the Web version.

The release build should not collect personal information unless a future service is explicitly added and disclosed before release. Any crash reporting, analytics, advertising, cloud sync, or purchases must be documented in the platform store privacy forms and this notice before enabling it.

For support or privacy questions, publish a project-owned contact address before submitting to a store.
