## Short description
Grow a joyful farm city, merge market goods, serve citizens, and uncover an ancient world below your land.

## Full description
Build the valley you have always imagined. Grow seasonal crops, care for friendly cattle, collect fresh goods, and merge ingredients into brilliant recipes. Trade with neighbors and travelers, earn coins and stars, then invest in community halls, restaurants, decorations, and breathtaking wonders that make citizens happier.

When the fields are thriving, descend into the Ancient City tunnels beneath the farm. Restore forgotten places, discover original relics, and unlock new recipes and town improvements. Merge & Munch is a warm, original farming-city adventure designed for short sessions and satisfying long-term progress.
